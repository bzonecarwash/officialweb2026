Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

BZONE Carwash & Auto Detailing ("BZONE," "we," "us") operates a Facebook Page and uses automated tools to respond to comments and messages sent to that Page. This policy explains what information is involved and how it is used.

What we collect

When you comment on our Facebook Page or send us a Messenger message, our system may read the text of that comment or message in order to generate a reply. We do not collect or store your personal information beyond what Facebook already provides through its Page and Messenger platforms (such as your name and message content, as visible to any Page admin).

How we use it

Comment and message content is used solely to generate a relevant reply — for example, answering a question about our services, pricing, or booking. We do not sell, share, or use this information for advertising or any purpose outside of responding to you.

Data retention

We do not maintain a separate database of your messages or comments outside of what is already stored within Facebook's own Messenger and Page systems.

Third-party services

Replies may be generated with the help of Anthropic's Claude AI and processed through Make.com automation. These services process message content solely to generate a reply and do not use it for any other purpose on our behalf.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us directly through our Facebook Page, BZONE Carwash & Auto Detailing, Hechanova St., La Paz, Iloilo City, Philippines.
